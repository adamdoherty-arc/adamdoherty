Wave

Wave is a personal assistant I built for my own Google account. It is not a public product: it is used by one person, me, and nobody else can sign in to it.

With my permission, Wave:

  • reads my Gmail to sort it, applying labels under Personal/ and archiving mail I no longer need in the inbox;
  • finds job-alert email and scores the postings against my resume;
  • reads my Google Calendar so my morning brief can list the day ahead.

Wave never sends, deletes or drafts email on my behalf, and never changes my calendar. It runs on my own computer, and my mail is processed there by a locally hosted language model. How it handles Google data is set out in the privacy policy.
